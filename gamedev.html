Game Development

I've always been interested in games and a couple of years ago, I started to learn how to make them. The gif shows the project from the latest course I finished. It was the 'Unity 3rd Person Combat and Traversal' course from GameDev.tv. Creating something from my imagination or scratch is hard. Too hard. I needed something to help make a foundation to refine and experiment. That turned out to be much more doable for my own learning.

I spent a while just trying out different options. The two I spent the most time on were Unreal Engine 5 (UE5) and Unity. I used a combination of C++ and visual scripting (Blueprints) for UE5 and mostly C# for Unity. That makes it sound like this would all go into the programming category , but a game engine is a giant web of different technologies and processes to get a game working.
